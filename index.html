Who We Are

Protection built on discipline, not shortcuts.

Jama Security is a professional, fully compliant South African security services provider delivering comprehensive, reliable and technology-driven protection to corporate, commercial, industrial, residential and public-sector clients.

Built on extensive industry experience, Jama combines physical guarding, advanced monitoring, investigations and AI-augmented security technology to deliver effective, cost-efficient and client-focused protection nationwide.

Our operations are guided by strong ethical principles, disciplined management and a commitment to service excellence — safeguarding people, property, assets and reputations while building long-term, trusted partnerships with our clients and communities.
